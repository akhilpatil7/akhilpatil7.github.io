Media Strategy & Geo Targeting

Deciding where to spend is a harder analytical problem than deciding how much. A market can look efficient simply because it's saturated, or look expensive simply because it's under-penetrated. These essays cover the decision-support machinery I build for media teams: normalized opportunity indices that make hundreds of markets comparable on one axis, weighted ranking frameworks for sub-market geo targeting, and significance testing to settle vendor and device questions with evidence instead of habit.

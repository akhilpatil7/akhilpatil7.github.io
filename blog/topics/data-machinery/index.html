Data Machinery & Operational Analytics

Most analytics failures are not modelling failures. They are foundation failures: the same customer counted three times across three systems, a funnel nobody can measure end to end, an alert nobody trusts because it cried wolf. These essays are about the unglamorous machinery underneath the insight — entity resolution across systems with no shared key, prospect-level datasets that make a real funnel measurable, automated cross-sell views, and statistical process control that fires when something is genuinely wrong.
